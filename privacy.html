ED Industrial CRM Action Privacy

This private action lets its authorized user read and update the ED Industrial CRM from ChatGPT.

Data processed

The action processes the CRM identifiers and field values supplied for the requested operation, such as company names, contacts, activity notes and follow-up tasks.

Use and storage

Requests are used only to perform the requested operation in ED Industrial's Supabase CRM. The action does not sell or share CRM data and does not maintain a separate marketing database.

Security

The action requires a private API credential and exposes bounded CRM operations rather than arbitrary database commands.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to salescontact@edindustrial.ca.
